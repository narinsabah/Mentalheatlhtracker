# Echo — Daily Mental Health & Micro-Journaling

Echo is a minimalist mental health tracker and micro-journal designed for emotional clarity and intentional self-care. Through daily flash prompts and a 50-word reflection constraint, it encourages quick, consistent self-awareness without the overwhelm of traditional journaling.

## ✨ Features

- **Daily Flash Prompts:** Thoughtful, rotating questions designed to encourage reflection.
- **50-Word Focus:** Encourages concise, honest thoughts and silences overthinking.
- **Mood Sentiment Detection:** Automatically identifies emotional tone from your words.
- **Weekly Mood Tracker:** Visual breakdown and summaries of your emotional patterns over the last 7 days.
- **Time Capsule Vault:** Stores past reflections locally and privately in your browser.
- **Dark / Light Mode:** Distraction-free, aesthetic interface.

## 🔒 Privacy

All entries and mood calculations are processed directly inside your browser and stored locally on your device via `localStorage`. No personal thoughts or reflections leave your machine.
